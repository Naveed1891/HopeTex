*   The time varies by country, but generally it takes **6–12 months** depending on examination, objections, or oppositions.

*   While you can file it yourself, many businesses prefer professional help to ensure proper classification, avoid rejection, and handle legal objections effectively.

*   
Typically, you need:

    *   Your business or personal details
    *   The logo or word mark
    *   List of goods/services (Trademark Class)
    *   Power of Attorney (if represented by an agent)

*   Trademark protection usually lasts **10 years** and can be renewed indefinitely as long as you continue using the mark and filing renewals on time.

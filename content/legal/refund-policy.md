## Nature of Our Services:

HopeTex Ltd provides professional consulting, document preparation, and filing assistance services. We are not a government authority and do not control government agencies, processing timelines, or approval decisions.

Because our services involve professional time, administrative work, compliance checks, and third-party coordination, refund eligibility depends on the stage of service delivery.

## Eligibility for Refund:

A refund may be considered under the following circumstances:

*   Duplicate payment made in error
*   Billing error or technical issue
*   Cancellation request submitted before any work has commenced
*   Refund requests must be submitted in writing through our **[Contact Form](https://hopetexx.com/contact-us/)** or via email.

Each case is reviewed individually at our sole discretion.

## Non-Refundable Services:

Refunds will not be issued under the following circumstances:

*   Work has already commenced
*   Documents have been prepared or drafted
*   Applications have been submitted to a government authority
*   Compliance review has been completed
*   The delay is caused by a government authority (IRS, Companies House, etc.)
*   The client provides incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information

Professional service fees become non-refundable once processing has begun.

## Government and Third-Party Fees:

Government filing fees, statutory charges, and third-party processing costs are strictly non-refundable once submitted or paid.

These include, but are not limited to:

*   State filing fees (LLC formation, Annual Reports, amendments, etc.)
*   Companies House fees (UK LTD registration, confirmation statements)
*   IRS application or processing fees (EIN, ITIN, etc.)
*   DUNS registration processing fees
*   Seller’s Permit filing fees
*   BOIR filing fees (if applicable)

These payments are made directly to government authorities or external regulatory bodies and are outside our control. As such, they cannot be refunded under any circumstances once processing has begun.

## Rejected Applications:

Approval decisions are made solely by the relevant government authorities.

If an application is rejected:

*   Government fees remain non-refundable
*   Service fees may be partially refundable only if no corrective re-submission is possible
*   We may offer re-submission assistance at no additional professional cost (excluding government fees), where applicable

We do not guarantee approval outcomes.

Errors caused by inaccurate or incomplete client information are not grounds for refund.

To request a refund, you must:

*   Submit a written request via our website contact form or email

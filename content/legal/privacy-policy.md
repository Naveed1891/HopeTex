**HopeTex Ltd** (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, including the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, HopeTex Ltd is the Data Controller of your personal data.

## Information We Collect:

We collect different types of information to provide and improve our services. The information we collect depends on how you interact with our website and services. This may include personal information you voluntarily provide as well as technical data collected automatically.

### Personal Information:

Personal information refers to data that can identify or contact an individual. We collect such information lawfully and in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR regulations. Your name and email address are examples of personal data we may collect.

This data is obtained through various means, including inquiry forms, incorporation applications, and account creation on our website portal for purchasing products and services. We strive to request only necessary information and ensure its security, accuracy, and currency through our secure servers with the latest security systems.

Personal information we may collect includes, but is not limited to:

*   First and Last name

*   Gender 

*   Date of birth 

*   Email address 

*   Telephone number 

*   Residential or business address 

*   Billing and credit/debit card information 

*   Professional information (occupation, position) 

### Non-Personal Information:

We may also collect certain non-identifiable technical information automatically when you visit our website. This information helps us understand user behavior and improve website performance. Such data may include:

*   IP address
*   Browser type
*   Device type
*   Operating system
*   Website usage data
*   Cookies and tracking data

## How We Collect Your Data:

We collect personal data through direct interactions with you and through automated technologies. Information is collected when you voluntarily provide it or when you use our website and services. This may occur when you:

*   Registering on our website
*   Purchasing or requesting services
*   Submitting contact forms
*   Communicating via email, phone, or WhatsApp
*   Providing billing details

## Legal Basis for Processing:

Under applicable data protection laws, including GDPR, we are required to identify the legal grounds on which we process your personal data. Depending on the circumstances, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

*   **Consent–** When you voluntarily provide information or agree to marketing communications
*   **Contractual necessity–** When processing is required to fulfill a service agreement
*   **Legal obligation–** When we must comply with regulatory or statutory requirements (e.g., AML compliance)
*   **Legitimate interests–** When processing is necessary for business operations, provided it does not override your rights

## How We Use Your Information:

We use the information collected to provide, manage, and improve our services. Processing is carried out in a lawful and transparent manner. Your information may be used to:

*   Provide business registration and compliance services
*   Process payments
*   Communicate service updates and confirmations
*   Fulfill contractual obligations
*   Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements
*   Improve website functionality and user experience

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies:

Cookies are small files that, with your permission, are placed on your computer’s hard drive to enhance your web experience. Our website uses traffic log cookies for statistical analysis, and you can choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings.

## Data Security:

We prioritize the security of your information and have implemented physical, technical, and managerial procedures to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

## Links to Other Websites and Third Parties:

Our website may contain links to external sites. Once you leave our site, we no longer have control over your information, and you should review the privacy policies of those external sites.

## Retention of Data:

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for providing services and complying with legal obligations. Usage data may be retained for internal analysis and security purposes.

## Transfer of Data:

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on computers outside your jurisdiction to fulfill services you’ve selected. We ensure secure data treatment and adherence to GDPR requirements during any transfer.

## Disclosure of Data:

Under certain circumstances, we may disclose personal data in response to legal requirements or to protect our rights, property, or the safety of users and the public.

## Your Data Protection Rights:

You have the right to:

*   Access your data
*   Request correction
